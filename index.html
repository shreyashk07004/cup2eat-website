    About the founder

    A delicious & sustainable innovation

    CUP2EAT started with a simple question: why should a 2-minute chai leave behind a cup that lasts for years? Founder Varun Khera set out to build a cup you can actually eat: tasty, sturdy, FSSAI-registered and affordable enough for every café in India. From college fest stalls to orders across the country, CUP2EAT is on a mission to make every sip zero-waste.

    Varun Khera Founder, CUP2EAT
